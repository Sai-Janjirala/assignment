A Novel AI Based Breast Cancer Screening

Breast cancer is the leading cause of cancer deaths in women today. According to the World Health Organization (WHO), one in every 12 women have the risk of a breast abnormality. Early diagnosis is very critical to decrease mortality rates. We, at NIRAMAI, have developed a novel artificial intelligence-based medical device to detect breast cancer at a much earlier stage than traditional methods or self-examination. Our solution is a low cost, accurate, automated, portable breast cancer screening tool that can be deployed in any primary clinic, hospital or used for outreach programs. The core technology of our solution has been developed using our patented AI / machine learning algorithms for reliable and accurate detection of breast cancer.

Non-invasive

Our thermal scanning based Breast Cancer Screening is radiation-free and non-invasive, requiring no incisions.

Privacy-Sensitive

During Breast Cancer Screening, nobody sees or touches you, and the procedure is completely painless.

High Accuracy

Our AI- based clinically validated Breast Cancer Screening offers best-in-class sensitivity and specificity, that is non-inferior to traditional methods.

Affordable

The cost of the equipment as well as operating expense is 10-20% of traditional screening methods.

Portable

The portable equipment can be used to conduct screening camps in rural areas and corporate offices.

All ages

Niramai Test works for women aged 18 to 80+ years. It does not depend upon the age or breast density.
